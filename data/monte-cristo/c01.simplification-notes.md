# Chapter 1 level notes: Simple, Advanced, Original

Rebuilt 2026-11-19. The earlier Plain and Guided modes were too close to each
other and to the original, so they are replaced by two authored rewrites. The
authoring rules are in `scripts/adaptation-brief.md`; the text and word lists
are in `c01.adaptations.json`.

## Simple (A2)

- A paragraph-by-paragraph retelling in the present tense: about 2,200 words
  against 3,260 in the original.
- 20 supported words for the whole chapter: armateur, capitaine, équipage,
  marin, second, pilote, ancre, voile, barque, marchandises, comptable,
  associé, maréchal, douane, fièvre, malheur, obéir, fiancée, drapeau, méchant.
- `c01.level-check.json`: no other content word is above A2 in FLELex. Nine
  words are accepted as transparent or as checker misses (danger, cabine,
  franc, chef, fils, mois, fois).
- Cut on purpose: the harbour geography, the sail and rigging orders, the
  burial detail, the Italian proverb, and the Canebière saying.

## Advanced (B2)

- The whole chapter in modern French, about 3,100 words. Passé simple kept;
  imperfect subjunctive, `ne … point` and obsolete senses modernised.
- Sail names and rigging orders use general words; `navire`, `bâtiment`,
  `armateur`, `matelot`, `mouillage` and `cargaison` are kept because they
  recur through the book.
- 83 supported words, including names and places.
- One reading choice to check: paragraph 24, *ne point escompter pour 25,000
  francs de bénéfice*, is rendered as "do not give up this voyage's profit for
  25,000 francs" (Advanced) and "you will earn more than 25,000 francs"
  (Simple).

## Original

- Unchanged EPUB text, no highlighting. Its word list now also has *gréé* and
  *arrimé*.

## What to watch while reading

- Simple: any sentence that still needs a second look, and any word outside
  the 20 that you had to guess.
- Advanced: does it feel clearly harder than Simple and clearly easier than
  Dumas? Which highlighted words did you not need?
